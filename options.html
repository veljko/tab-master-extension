New tab position

Positioning never activates, reloads, or recreates a tab. Pinned and grouped tabs are left in the browser's chosen position. Immediately after browser startup, automatic positioning is temporarily suppressed while the session restore activity settles.

Keyboard shortcuts

Quick MRU switch: Alt+W

Move to first and last do not have default shortcuts. Assign keys that do not conflict with browser commands at edge://extensions/shortcuts or chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

All commands can be assigned or changed in the browser's extension shortcut settings.
